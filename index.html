Our Story

Celebrating Over 50 Years In Business!

Patrick Shoes Limited, established in the 1970s, is proud to celebrate half a century of excellence in the footwear industry. Based in Crick, Northamptonshire, the company has built a reputation for craftsmanship, durability, and innovative design over the decades.

Since its inception, Patrick Shoes Ltd has been synonymous with high-quality footwear. Each pair of shoes is crafted with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that customers receive products that are not only stylish but also long-lasting. This commitment to excellence has been the cornerstone of the company's success.
